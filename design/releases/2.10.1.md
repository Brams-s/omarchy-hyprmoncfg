# hyprmoncfg panel 2.10.1

One click on `+` or `−` now changes the workspace count or group size by exactly one. Requires hyprmoncfg 1.24.0 or newer; [1.25.1](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.25.1) is recommended.

The steppers look as before, so there is no new screenshot.

## Fixed

- **The WORKSPACES and GROUP SIZE steppers stop when you let go.** A single click kept adding workspaces, or growing the group size, every tenth of a second until the shell was restarted. The fields are disabled while an edit is pending, and disabling a held button skipped the release that stops the hold-to-repeat timer. The timers now stop as soon as the button is disabled. By @haraldan in [#39](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-hyprmoncfg/pull/39); reported by @haraldan in [#38](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-hyprmoncfg/issues/38).

## Known limitations

- **Holding `+` or `−` on WORKSPACES and GROUP SIZE steps once.** Their first step ends the press, so they don't repeat while held. Type the number instead to make a large change. Other steppers repeat while held as before.

## What's tested

All 219 Node tests, 21 panel QML tests and 21 bar QML tests pass, along with qmllint and plugin validation. The new regression test fails before the fix (one click made 6 steps) and passes after it, on Qt 6.12 and on Qt 6.4 in CI. @haraldan checked the WORKSPACES stepper in a live panel.
