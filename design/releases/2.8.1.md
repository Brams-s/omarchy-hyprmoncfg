# hyprmoncfg panel 2.8.1

The panel's text fields no longer read the clipboard on their own. They read it only when you paste. It works with [hyprmoncfg 1.19.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.19.0) or newer; [1.23.0](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v1.23.0) is recommended.

The fields look and behave as before, so there is no new screenshot.

## Fixed

- **Copying something in another app no longer makes the shell read it.** The bar keeps the panel loaded, and Qt's own text fields fetch the whole clipboard every time it changes, even while hidden and without a paste. A very large or slow clipboard could stall or exhaust the shared shell. The profile name, post-apply command, ICC path, position, colour and workspace fields are now drawn by the panel itself from key presses, and none of Qt's text inputs is left. Reported by @HANCORE-linux in the marketplace review ([omacom/omarchy-plugin-marketplace#11391](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-plugin-marketplace/issues/11391)).
- **Paste is explicit and bounded.** `Ctrl+V` or `Shift+Insert` runs `wl-paste` for at most two seconds and keeps the first 4096 bytes, as one line without control characters.

## Known limitations

- **No right-click menu or drag-to-select-a-word in the fields.** Typing, the arrows (with `Ctrl` by word and `Shift` to select), Home/End, Backspace/Delete, `Ctrl+A`, click and drag to select, double-click to select all, Enter and paste work. There is no Copy or Cut.
- **No input method support.** Text entered through an input method or a compose sequence does not reach the fields.

## What's tested

Node tests (194), offscreen QML tests (21), a new offscreen test of the field and the workspace stepper against stub shell modules (11), and plugin validation pass. Before and after captures of the real panel with sample displays, rendered offscreen, match except for the caret. Typing in the fields on a live desktop hasn't been tried yet.
